help Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions, Clear Answers.

What types of PCBs do you design? add

We design a full range of PCBs including single-sided, double-sided, and multi-layer boards. Our speciality is medical-grade PCBs with strict requirements for isolation, EMC compliance, thermal management, and reliability. We work with both rigid and flexible PCB technologies.

What is the typical project timeline? add

Timelines vary based on project complexity. A simple PCB design typically takes 2–4 weeks. More complex embedded systems or medical devices can take 3–6 months from concept to validated prototype. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.

Do you offer prototyping services? add

Yes. Rapid prototyping is one of our core services. We can produce functional prototypes within days using advanced manufacturing techniques, allowing you to validate designs quickly before committing to full production runs.

What industries do you serve? add

Our primary focus is medical devices and healthcare technology. We also serve industrial automation, biomedical research, robotics, and advanced technology sectors. Any application requiring precision electronic systems is within our scope.

How do you ensure quality and regulatory compliance? add

We follow rigorous engineering practices including design reviews, simulation-based validation, comprehensive testing (electrical, thermal, EMC), and full documentation. For medical applications, we design with regulatory frameworks like IEC 60601 and ISO 13485 in mind from the outset.

Is there a minimum order quantity? add

No. We support projects at every scale — from single prototypes to production runs of thousands of units. Whether you need one custom board or ongoing supply, we're equipped to help.

Do you sign NDAs for confidential projects? add

Absolutely. We understand the sensitivity of medical and proprietary technology development. We're happy to sign NDAs before any detailed technical discussions begin.

Can you work with our existing engineering team? add

Yes. We frequently collaborate as an extension of our clients' in-house teams. We can integrate into your workflows, use your preferred tools and documentation standards, and provide specialised expertise where needed.
